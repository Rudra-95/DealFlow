TASK 16 — Implement Quote Calculation Engine

Create:

services/quoteService.js

Implement:

subtotal
discount amount
line total
grand total
cost
margin

Formula:

gross = price × quantity

discount = gross × discount% / 100

net = gross - discount

margin = net - cost

Important: calculate these on the backend. Never trust totals coming from React.

The problem statement specifically gives this kind of example.

TASK 19 — Implement Blended Risk Score

Create:

calculateBlendedRisk()

Your system should consider all lines, not just the worst line.

Example:

Line 1 → +2 risk
Line 2 → +3 risk
Line 3 → +2 risk

Total blended risk → 7

This is important because the specification explicitly says small violations across multiple lines should accumulate into the blended risk.
